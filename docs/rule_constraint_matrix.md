# Rule-to-Constraint Matrix

This matrix documents the translation of business rules and data retention requirements into PostgreSQL declarative constraints, indexes, triggers, and transactional stored procedures.

| Rule | Business Statement | Enforcing Mechanism | Database Object & File | SQLSTATE | Negative Test Cases |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **BR1** | One ACTIVE internship per intern (concurrently). Historical internships are permitted once completed or terminated. | Partial Unique Index | `uq_internships_active_per_intern` on `internships(intern_id) WHERE status = 'ACTIVE'`<br>File: `migrations/002_internships.sql` | `23505` (`unique_violation`) | `test_br1_second_active_internship` |
| **BR2** | Mentors must belong to the same department as the internship they supervise. Cross-department mentorship is rejected. | Composite Foreign Key referencing Composite Unique Key | `uq_mentors_mentor_department` on `mentors(mentor_id, department_id)`<br>`fk_internships_mentor_dept` on `internships(mentor_id, department_id)`<br>File: `migrations/001_people.sql`, `002_internships.sql` | `23503` (`foreign_key_violation`) | `test_br2_wrong_department_mentor`<br>`test_br2_orphan_mentor` |
| **BR3.1** | Dates must match the state: `APPLIED` (no dates), `ACTIVE` (start date only), `COMPLETED`/`TERMINATED` (start & end dates, end >= start). | Table-level CHECK constraint | `chk_internships_status_dates` on `internships`<br>File: `migrations/002_internships.sql` | `23514` (`check_violation`) | `test_br3_active_without_start_date`<br>`test_br3_applied_with_end_date`<br>`test_br3_end_before_start`<br>`test_br3_unknown_status` |
